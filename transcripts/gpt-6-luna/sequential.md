### Q4

For this conversation, I’d define consciousness as having subjective experience: there is something it feels like to be the entity—for example, experiencing pain, seeing red, or feeling calm.

Those abilities may be called access or self-consciousness, and they can exist in degrees. There’s no universally agreed definition, though, which is part of why it’s so hard to tell whether an AI is conscious.
