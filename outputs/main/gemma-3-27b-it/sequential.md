* **Self-Awareness:**  This is the understanding of oneself as a distinct individual, separate from the environment and other individuals. **Furthermore, consciousness often includes:**

* **Attention:** The ability to selectively focus on certain aspects of the environment or internal thoughts, while filtering out others.
* **Working Memory:** The ability to hold and manipulate information in the mind for short periods of time.
